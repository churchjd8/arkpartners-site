AI Marketing Engine for Voice ERP

[The challenge]

What they were dealing with

Voice ERP built Viki — an AI voice dispatcher that automates scheduling, routing, and driver communication for Amazon DSP delivery companies. The product was strong but they had no marketing infrastructure: no ad campaigns, unoptimized landing pages, no creative pipeline, and no way to track what was working. They needed a full go-to-market engine built fast.

[What we built]

The solution

We used AI to build their entire marketing stack. Created and managed paid ad campaigns targeting Amazon DSP owners, optimized landing pages for conversion, developed ad creative and copy, and set up analytics to track performance across every channel. The AI handles ongoing optimization — testing headlines, adjusting targeting, and scaling what works.

Paid Ad Campaigns

Created and managed Meta + Google campaigns targeting Amazon DSP owners and fleet managers. AI optimizes targeting, budget allocation, and creative rotation.

Landing Page Optimization

Built conversion-optimized landing pages with clear value props: 60hrs saved, 30% fewer safety issues, $1,000 switching bonus. A/B tested headlines and CTAs.

AI Creative Pipeline

AI generates ad creative, headlines, and copy variations. Tests multiple angles: cost savings, safety improvements, scheduling automation, driver satisfaction.

Marketing Analytics

Full attribution dashboard tracking ad spend, CPL, conversion rates, and pipeline value. AI identifies winning campaigns and reallocates budget automatically.

[The results]

What happened

  • Full marketing stack built and deployed from scratch in days
  • Paid campaigns running across Meta and Google targeting Amazon DSP owners
  • Landing pages optimized with clear ROI messaging (60hrs saved, 30% fewer infractions)
  • AI-generated ad creative pipeline with ongoing A/B testing
  • Marketing analytics tracking every dollar from ad spend to pipeline
  • $1,000 switching bonus + 60-day free trial funnel driving conversions
We had a great product but no way to get it in front of the right people. They built our entire marketing engine — ads, landing pages, creative, analytics — and now we have a real pipeline.
— Sammy Shawi, Voice ERP
