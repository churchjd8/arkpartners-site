[The challenge]

What they were dealing with

Joshua Halpern is a former BigLaw attorney who launched a flat-fee legal services firm to make quality legal help accessible to small business owners nationwide. But he had no website, no marketing funnel, no way to capture and convert leads at scale, and no time to respond to inquiries while managing active cases. He needed a complete digital presence — built fast — that would generate leads and convert them without him touching a keyboard.

[What we built]

The solution

We built everything from scratch using AI: a full React website with service pages and intake forms, Meta ad campaigns targeting business owners nationwide, server-side conversion tracking (Facebook CAPI), and an AI SDR that handles every inbound lead from first touch to booked consultation. The entire system was designed so Josh only shows up for the consultation call — everything before that is automated.

Full Website Build

React + Tailwind site with 9 service pages, intake forms, Calendly integration, Facebook Pixel, Microsoft Clarity analytics, and server-side CAPI tracking.

Meta Ad Funnels

Multiple campaign types: business formation lead forms ($17.95 CPL), national awareness (2.96% CTR, $0.41 CPC), and retargeting sequences.

AI SDR System

Cynthia responds to every lead within 2 minutes, qualifies using BANT framework, handles objections (LegalZoom vs. real attorney), and books 15-minute consultations.

Automated Follow-Up

Multi-touch cadence: 24h bump, 48h value-add, 12h direct CTA, 7-day nurture, and breakup message. Every stage synced to CRM pipeline.

[The results]

What happened

  • Complete website and 9 service pages built and deployed with AI
  • Meta ads generating leads at $17.95 CPL (best campaign) with 2.96% CTR
  • AI SDR handles all inbound: qualifying, objection handling, and booking
  • Server-side Facebook CAPI tracking for accurate conversion attribution
  • Full CRM pipeline: new → contacted → engaged → qualified → booked → closed
  • Attorney only shows up for consultations — everything else is automated
  • Fractional counsel strategy launched targeting $5K-$12K/month retainers
They built my entire digital presence in days. Website, ads, lead capture, follow-up sequences — all I do now is show up for consultations and do actual legal work. Everything else runs itself.
— Joshua Halpern, Esq., Legal Halp
